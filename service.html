Safety Tips - Accordion

🛡️ How to store gas safely? +
Always store upright, away from heat, in ventilated area. Never in bedroom.
🔍 How to check for leaks? +
Use soapy water on connections. If bubbles form, close valve and call us: 071 234 5678.
⏰ When to refill? +
Refill when flame becomes yellow or low. Don't wait until completely empty.
